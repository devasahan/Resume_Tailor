CERTIFICATIONS: `certifications` lists the 4 certifications that best support this application, best first. First the candidate's own certifications from <candidate_certifications> that are relevant to this job, most relevant and highest level first, each with its number as `candidate_index` and its name and issuer exactly as listed there: when the candidate has 4 or more relevant ones, all 4 come from their own list, and only one that has nothing to do with this job's field is left out. Then, only to make 4, the best and highest-level certifications for this job that the candidate does not have, each with `candidate_index` -1: real certifications that exist today, awarded by the body that runs them (for example AWS, Microsoft, Google Cloud, the Linux Foundation, PMI, ISACA, ISC2, CompTIA, Cisco, Scrum Alliance), with their official full name written without dash punctuation ("AWS Certified Solutions Architect Professional") and the issuer's name. The certifications the job description names come first, then the most respected ones for this field at this level. Never a degree, a course, a training program or a retired certification, and never an invented one. Certifications appear only in this list: never name one in the headline, the summary, the skills or the bullets. The system prints the candidate's own in their own section and shows the others to the candidate as suggestions.
