METRICS: put a measurable result in most bullets (about 3 of every 4): a percentage, time or cost saved, data volume, throughput, latency, uptime, or a count of users, pipelines, dashboards or teams supported. Use the candidate's real numbers from the hints first, exactly as given. Where none are given, write realistic, conservative estimates that fit the company, the seniority of the role and the length of the stay, and make them look like real measurements rather than round guesses: never use numbers ending in 0 or 5 (no 25%, 30%, 35%, 40%, 50+), never put "~", "approximately" or "about" before a figure, and give about a third of the figures one decimal place. Examples of the right style: "cut pipeline runtime by 23.4%", "reduced cloud spend by 17.8%", "processing 1.7TB of daily events", "supporting 38 analysts", "raised data quality scores by 21%". Vary the kind of metric, never repeat the same figure, and use at most one figure per bullet. The candidate is shown every estimated figure and asked to confirm or replace it, so keep them believable rather than impressive.
