Product Marketing Manager, B2B SaaS

You will shape how our workflow software is positioned, launched and sold. You work closely with product, sales and customer success and own the story from first message to renewal.

Responsibilities
- Develop positioning, messaging and pricing inputs for new features and segments.
- Plan and run product launches across email, web, events and sales enablement.
- Build sales collateral, battlecards and demo narratives; train account executives.
- Run customer interviews and win/loss analysis and share insights with product and leadership.
- Track launch performance in HubSpot and Looker and report on pipeline influence.
- Manage a small budget and coordinate agencies and freelancers.

Requirements
- 4+ years in product marketing or a related B2B role.
- Excellent writing and storytelling, strong stakeholder management and project management skills.
- Comfort with analytics, A/B testing and customer research. Salesforce experience preferred.
