Customer Success Manager

We help mid-market customers get lasting value from our platform. As a Customer Success Manager you will manage a portfolio of accounts, drive adoption and renewals, and be the voice of the customer inside the company.

What you will do
- Onboard new customers, run kickoff calls and build success plans with clear milestones.
- Monitor product usage and health scores in Salesforce and Zendesk; spot risk early and act on it.
- Lead quarterly business reviews and present outcomes to executives.
- Partner with support, product and sales to resolve issues and shape the roadmap.
- Own renewals and identify expansion opportunities with account executives.
- Document best practices and help improve our onboarding playbook.

You bring
- 3+ years in customer success, account management or support for a SaaS product.
- Strong communication, negotiation and problem solving skills; comfortable presenting to senior stakeholders.
- Familiarity with CRM tools, reporting in Excel or Tableau, and agile ways of working.
